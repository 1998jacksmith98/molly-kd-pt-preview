Foot placement can completely change the muscle focus of this machine.

Having your feet placed on the top footpad allows greater knee flexion which shortens the hamstrings at the knee, meaning they are less lengthened throughout the movement. This shifts more emphasis towards the glutes.

Having your feet placed under the foot padding allows for more of a open knee angle keeping the hamstrings in a more lengthened position while still allowing plenty of hip extension, giving the hamstrings as-well as the glutes more opportunity to be trained.

So if your goal is more glute and hamstring involvement, try the lower foot position.
For more glute bias, try the higher position.

Why barbell squats are often a better option for progressive overload than dumbbell squats
With dumbbell squats, grip strength and the ability to hold heavy weights at your sides can become the limiting factor before your lower body is fully challenged.
Barbell squats allow the load to be distributed more efficiently across the body, making it easier to progressively increase weight over time while maintaining stability and proper squat mechanics.
For long-term lower body strength and muscle growth, this makes barbell squats a more effective option for progressive overload.
